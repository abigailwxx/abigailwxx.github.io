EmoJinary
EMOJINARY combines interactive web design and collectible phone cases to bridge cultural differences in emoji interpretation and promote cross-cultural communication.
ROLE
Designer
YEAR
2025
TOOLS
Illustrator, Cargo, Photoshop
CATEGORY
Branding Campaign & Web Design
Phone Cases & Poster Design
The campaign’s physical output consists of collectible phone cases featuring texting-style dialogues, pixelated visuals, grainy gradient textures, and intentionally mismatched emoji meanings. By transforming cultural misinterpretation into a fashionable, shareable object that people actually use, the phone cases create curiosity, humor, and social visibility in everyday contexts.
Interactive Website & Digital System
The digital component of EMOJINARY is an interactive emoji-translation website that allows users to explore American, Chinese, and universal interpretations of commonly used emojis. Designed as an educational yet playful platform, the website expands on the physical products by providing cultural context, explanations, and comparisons. Through interaction and discovery, users are encouraged to reflect on their own texting habits and become more mindful communicators across cultures.
