KRAKK Tropic Thump
A playful, tropical-inspired beer brand that combines vibrant packaging and interactive QR codes to transform conventional beer packaging into an engaging experience that encourages consumer interaction and brand discovery.
ROLE
Designer, with Lexi Cao
YEAR
2026
TOOLS
Illustrator, Cargo, Photoshop
CATEGORY
Branding Campaign & Web Design
Project Overview
This collaborative branding project began with a set of randomized creative constraints designed to simulate a real-world design challenge: toucans as the subject, beer lovers as the audience, music as inspiration, typography as the primary design element, and QR codes as the medium. Inspired by tropical rainforest music and the vibrant character of toucans, we developed a playful, neo-tropical beer brand centered on color, rhythm, and movement.
Brand Identity & Interactive Packaging
Drawing from tropical colors, musical rhythms, and the distinctive character of toucans, the designs establish a lively and recognizable brand system. QR codes serve as both functional and visual elements within the packaging. By incorporating a mix of real and decoy QR codes, the design transforms an ordinary product interaction into a playful discovery experience, encouraging consumers to explore the packaging and find the hidden reward.
Try it on your phone.
Website & Digital Experience
QR CODES
The mobile website extends the brand experience beyond the physical product. Consumers who discover and scan the real QR code are directed to a prize page, where they unlock an exclusive new beer flavor. Through interactive storytelling and product discovery, the website connects the physical packaging with a digital experience, turning a simple QR code scan into an opportunity for engagement and reinforcing the brand's playful identity.
