01. Branding
Made In China Festival
A brand identity for Made In China, a Chinese hip-hop festival. Local festival graphics often look generic and flat, so the identity fuses calligraphy and seal carving with chrome 3D forms to give Chinese rap a distinct visual voice.
ROLE
Designer
YEAR
2024
TOOLS
Illustrator, Rhino, Photoshop
CATEGORY
Festival Identity
Collateral
The system extends to every touchpoint, from staff, media, and guest passes shaped as folding fans to tickets, wristbands, invitations, and stickers.
Posters
Two posters anchor the identity: a key visual built around the 3D logo, and a lineup poster that spreads the artists across an open folding fan. The palette draws on soft tones from traditional Chinese painting.
Festival Kit
Every piece comes together in a festival kit: posters, tickets, wristbands, ID cards, stickers, and a hand-rolled scroll invitation tied with ribbon, presented as one complete package.

Rooted in Chinese calligraphy and seal carving: a seal-script logo, modeled in 3D and rendered in chrome to carry an ancient craft into the present.
