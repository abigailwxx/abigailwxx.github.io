Wireless Festival is an established annual music festival in London, known for bringing together major artists in hip-hop, rap, R&B, and contemporary urban music. This project reimagines the festival’s visual identity through a bold, energetic typographic system that reflects the intensity and atmosphere of live performances. The redesigned identity combines expressive, distorted typography, high-contrast colors, glowing gradients, and dynamic diagonal compositions inspired by stage lighting and sound.

Building on the visual identity, the project extends the rebrand into a digital experience through a redesigned festival website. The website translates the expressive graphic language into an interactive system, using oversized typography, angled layouts, and luminous gradients to create a sense of movement and energy. The landing page introduces the festival and its atmosphere, while the lineup page organizes artists and performances through a clear visual hierarchy and day-based navigation. The design balances expressive visual storytelling with functional information architecture, making it easier for visitors to explore the lineup, discover artists, and navigate essential festival information.
