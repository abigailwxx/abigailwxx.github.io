Exhibition Identity
Exhibition Identity
The visual identity for this exhibition draws inspiration from the architecture of the exhibition space itself. Window structures and shifting perspectives became key visual elements, reflecting both the physical environment and the way individual works are viewed within a shared context. The exhibition showcases projects from all sophomore Graphic Design students. A vibrant spectrum of colors was incorporated throughout the visual language to emphasize the diversity of ideas, approaches, and outcomes represented in the exhibition.
Exhibition Installation & Opening
Developed collaboratively within a fast-paced, one-week timeline, the exhibition identity balanced structure and experimentation, creating a cohesive visual system that celebrated individuality while unifying the work under a shared concept.
